9. Create a Product Review and Rating System in MongoDB. Store customer reviews and use aggregation to calculate the average rating and number of reviews for each product.

use CollegeDB
db.reviews.insertMany([
 {productId:101,customer:"Aman",rating:5,review:"Good"},
 {productId:101,customer:"Sara",rating:4,review:"Nice"},
 {productId:102,customer:"Ali",rating:3,review:"Average"}
])
db.reviews.aggregate([
 {$group:{_id:"$productId",averageRating:{$avg:"$rating"},totalReviews:{$sum:1}}}
])
