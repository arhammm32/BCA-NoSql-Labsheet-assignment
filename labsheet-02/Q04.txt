4. Design a Flexible Product Catalog where laptops, mobiles, and cameras have different sets of attributes. Store them in a common collection and write queries to search category-specific properties.

use CollegeDB
db.products.insertMany([
 {name:"Laptop",category:"laptop",ram:16,brand:"HP"},
 {name:"Phone",category:"mobile",storage:128,brand:"Samsung"},
 {name:"Camera",category:"camera",megapixels:24,brand:"Canon"}
])
db.products.find({category:"laptop",ram:{$gte:8}})
db.products.find({category:"mobile",storage:128})
db.products.find({category:"camera",megapixels:{$gte:20}})
