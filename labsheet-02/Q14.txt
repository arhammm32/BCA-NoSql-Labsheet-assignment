14. Create a Real-Time Order Tracking System where an order moves through stages such as Placed, Confirmed, Shipped, and Delivered. Store the complete status history and retrieve the current status of an order.

use CollegeDB
db.tracking.insertOne({
 orderId:101,currentStatus:"Placed",
 history:[{status:"Placed",time:new Date()}]
})
db.tracking.updateOne({orderId:101},{$set:{currentStatus:"Confirmed"},$push:{history:{status:"Confirmed",time:new Date()}}})
db.tracking.updateOne({orderId:101},{$set:{currentStatus:"Shipped"},$push:{history:{status:"Shipped",time:new Date()}}})
db.tracking.findOne({orderId:101},{currentStatus:1,history:1})
