Q7. Create an HBase table named WebsiteCounter to maintain page-view counters. Implement increment operations and display the updated counter values for different pages.

create 'WebsiteCounter', 'views'
incr 'WebsiteCounter', 'home', 'views:count', 1
incr 'WebsiteCounter', 'home', 'views:count', 1
incr 'WebsiteCounter', 'about', 'views:count', 1
get 'WebsiteCounter', 'home'
get 'WebsiteCounter', 'about'
scan 'WebsiteCounter'
